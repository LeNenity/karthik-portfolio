01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

A lunar outpost with limited resources.

Outpost 07 was developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge. Our goal was to make decisions about a Moon base tangible for young players. Instead of reading about space exploration, players make choices about power, water, oxygen, food and shelter.

Daily tasks prepare the crew for the following night, with emergencies exposing the cost of incomplete work. The game also includes mission cinematics.

02 / GAMEPLAY

Explore the base. Keep the crew going.

Players collect materials, process resources, grow food and maintain equipment while balancing their supplies.

03 / GAME DESIGN

Every system affects another.

The game makes resource decisions matter. Running one system may consume resources needed elsewhere, so preparation and teamwork make a difference.

Life supportOxygen leaks need repairs before night.
Radiation shieldingRepairs compete with upgrades for scrap.
PowerProcessing ice and growing food draw energy.
FoodGreenhouse production uses water and electricity.
CommunicationMessages model a delay between Earth and the Moon.
Emergency reserveSupporting one resource consumes another.

04 / DATA INTEGRATION

Real space-weather data in the game.

Roblox HttpService requests recent solar-flare data from NASA’s DONKI database. Flare classifications affect the severity of the storms on nights five and seven. When requests fail, the game uses a clearly labelled historical archive rather than presenting stored records as live data.

05 / LEARNING & EXPLORATION

Learning through the mission.

Captain Nova provides Moon facts, and short end-of-day quizzes reinforce what players encounter. The buggy and rover add ways to explore the setting.

06 / TEAM & RECOGNITION
